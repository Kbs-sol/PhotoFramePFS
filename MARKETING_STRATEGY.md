## 2. Market Opportunity

### Market Size
- India home décor market: ~₹30,000 crore (2024), growing at 10% CAGR
- Online home décor segment: ~₹5,000 crore, growing faster
- Photo frames & wall art sub-segment: ~₹2,000 crore
- Addressable market for premium niche prints: ₹200–400 crore, largely untapped

### Consumer Trends Driving Demand
1. **Social media home aesthetics**: Instagram/Pinterest-driven décor culture in Indian millennials
2. **Work-from-home office setups**: Home office wall art becoming standard
3. **Rising disposable income**: Tier-2 cities with aspiring middle class
4. **Gifting shift**: Practical → experiential → aesthetic gifts
5. **Fandom culture growth**: Football (Messi/CR7), anime, F1 fandom exploding in India
6. **Spiritual home décor**: Pooja rooms and devotional art remain evergreen

## 3. Competitor Analysis

### 3.1 Candid Apricot (candidapricot.com) — Primary Competitor

**Positioning**: Custom photo frames, wedding albums, personalised prints  
**Price Range**: ₹500–₹5,000+  
**Strengths**:
- Established brand (~5+ years)
- Strong wedding/personalised gifting niche
- Good Google search presence for "personalised photo frames India"
- Multiple product types (books, calendars, frames, mugs)

**Weaknesses**:
- No dedicated niche art catalog (generic/personalised only)
- No spiritual, sports, automotive, or anime category
- Website UX is dated — not mobile-first
- No AI/SEO discoverability features
- Heavy on personalisation, light on curated art

**ChitraFrame Advantage vs Candid Apricot**:
- We have 51 curated designs across 6 passion-identity categories
- Faster mobile checkout (single-page)
- Better pricing transparency (all sizes visible upfront)
- Superior SEO: blog posts, llms.txt, JSON-LD structured data
- Instagram-native brand (Candid Apricot is not reels-focused)

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Price Architecture
| Size | Dimensions | Framed | Poster | With White Mount |
|------|-----------|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Small | 8×12" | ₹499 | ₹299 | ₹749 framed |
| Medium | 12×18" | ₹799 | ₹499 | ₹1,049 framed |
| Large | 18×24" | ₹1,149 | ₹799 | ₹1,399 framed |
| XL | 24×36" | ₹1,749 | ₹1,249 | ₹1,999 framed |

### Pricing Rationale
- **Small (₹499 framed)**: Entry-level impulse buy — under ₹500 psychological threshold
- **Medium (₹799 framed)**: Most popular — balances size vs price for millennials
- **Large (₹1,149 framed)**: Statement piece tier, gifting sweet spot
- **XL (₹1,749 framed)**: Premium, wow-factor piece for dedicated fans or statement walls
- **Poster ~40% cheaper**: For those wanting print-only; also drives up order value when buying multiple

### Revenue Drivers
- Average Order Value (AOV) target: ₹899+ (to trigger free shipping, naturally)
- Upsell levers: White mount (+₹250), size upgrade, multiple prints bundle
- Repeat purchase: Seasonal designs, new catalog drops every quarter

### Discounts & Promotions
- **Prepaid discount**: ₹50 off (always active — no COD margin loss)
- **Free shipping**: ₹899+ (pushes AOV up naturally)
- **Festival promos**: 10–15% off during Diwali, Holi, Valentine's (limited time, banner-driven)
- **Bundle offers**: "Buy 2 get 10% off" (planned Q2)
- **No deep discounting**: Maintaining premium positioning; avoiding race to ₹99

## 12. Risk Analysis

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Low initial traffic | High | High | Instagram organic + SEO blog posts (already done) |
| Competitors copy designs | Medium | Medium | Move fast, add new designs quarterly |
| Payment failures / UX drop-off | Medium | High | Razorpay integration testing, cart abandonment flow |
| Shipping damage complaints | Medium | High | Premium packaging, clear 48hr return policy |
| IP concerns on sports/anime designs | Low-Medium | High | Consult IP lawyer; use stylized/illustrated, not photo reproductions |
| Festival demand surge → stockout | Low | Medium | Print-on-demand model = no inventory risk |
| Ad account ban (Meta) | Low | Medium | Keep organic content primary, diversify channels |
