Why Framed Anime Art is Better Than Posters

Most anime fans in India have bought paper posters at some point — and most have been disappointed. Here's why framed art prints are worth the upgrade:

  • Quality: 250gsm art paper vs thin paper. The difference is enormous.
  • Longevity: Glass-fronted frame protects against dust, moisture and UV fading.
  • Appearance: A framed print looks like gallery art. A paper poster looks like a paper poster.
  • Respect for the art: Your favourite characters deserve better than a cheap print.
