How to Decorate Your Living Room with Framed Art — India 2025 Guide

Transform bare walls into beautiful statements. A room-by-room guide for Indian homes.

The living room is where your home makes its first impression — on guests, on family, and on yourself every day. Framed wall art is one of the most effective (and affordable) ways to elevate the look and feel of a space. Here's your complete guide to doing it right.

Rule #1 — Choose the Right Size

Size is the most common mistake people make with wall art. Here's a quick guide:

Size Dimensions Best For Framed Price
Small8×12"Desks, small walls, gallery walls₹499
Medium12×18"Bedrooms, hallways, standard walls₹799
Large18×24"Living rooms, feature walls₹1,149
XL24×36"Statement walls, large rooms₹1,749

General rule: The art should fill 2/3 to 3/4 of the wall width above a sofa or console. For a 6-foot sofa, Large (18×24") is the minimum — XL (24×36") is ideal.

Rule #2 — Hang at Eye Level

The centre of your art should be at eye level — approximately 57–60 inches from the floor. This is the gallery standard used worldwide. When hanging above a sofa, leave 6–8 inches between the top of the sofa and the bottom of the frame.

Rule #3 — Choose Art That Matches Your Home's Energy

Traditional Indian Homes

Spiritual art works beautifully — Ram Darbar Classic, Radha Krishna Watercolor Garden or Mahadev Cosmic Trance. Rich colours and devotional subjects harmonise perfectly with traditional décor, warm lighting and wooden furniture.

Modern/Contemporary Homes

Go for bold, graphic pieces. Mahadev Neon Dark Minimal, Lion Geometric Gold Portrait or Midnight Lambo Neon are perfect for modern interiors with white walls and clean lines.

Eclectic/Boho Homes

Beach Sunset Oil Paint, Wolf Forest Spirit or Woman Line Art Gold fit the eclectic aesthetic. These designs have a free-spirited, artistic quality that suits boho interiors.

Minimalist Homes

One hero piece is better than many small ones. Choose a large format (18×24" or 24×36") with a bold, clean design. Discipline = Freedom (typographic), Wolf Alpha Dark or Goku Ultra Instinct work well in minimal spaces.

Gallery Wall Ideas for Indian Homes

A gallery wall with 3–5 coordinated pieces creates a stunning feature wall. Some combinations that work beautifully:

  • Spiritual gallery: Mahadev Cosmic Trance (Large centre) + Ganesha Gold Mandala + Radha Krishna Teal & Gold
  • Sports gallery: Messi World Cup Glory (Large centre) + CR7 Golden Era + F1 Red Bull Sparks
  • Wildlife gallery: Lion Geometric Gold (Large centre) + Wolf Moonlight + Wolf Forest Spirit
  • Automotive gallery: Porsche 911 Neon Tokyo (Large centre) + BMW M4 Dark Carbon + F1 Speed Art

The White Mount Upgrade

For just ₹250 extra, add a White Mount (mat board) inside your frame. This creates a gallery-quality border around your print, making even a small print look museum-worthy. Highly recommended for living room pieces where you want maximum impact.

Lighting Your Wall Art

Good lighting dramatically enhances wall art. Options for Indian homes:

  • Track lighting: Most versatile, can be angled precisely at artwork
  • Picture light (battery): Mounts on or above the frame, no wiring needed
  • Warm ambient light: A warm floor lamp nearby creates beautiful atmosphere
