Where to Place Sports Art

Sports/Game Room

Go big. XL (24×36", ₹1,749) or Large (18×24", ₹1,149) framed prints create an immersive sports environment. Consider a gallery wall with 2–3 complementary designs.

Gym / Home Workout Space

Sports art in a gym serves as daily motivation. Medium (12×18", ₹799) at eye level while training is optimal. Motivational typographic art pairs perfectly with sports prints.

Bedroom

A single hero piece — Medium or Large — above the headboard. The print should inspire, not overwhelm. Go for a design that speaks to your deepest passion.

Office/Study

Sports art in an office signals personality and drive. Small (8×12", ₹499) to Medium works well on a side wall without dominating the space.

Framed vs Poster — Which to Choose?

For sports art, we generally recommend the Framed option. Sports prints tend to be vibrant and bold — a frame protects them, enhances the colours and makes them look premium. The glass front also prevents fading over time.

Choose Poster (unframed) only if you already have a favourite frame at home, or prefer to choose your own frame colour.
