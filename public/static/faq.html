Products & Sizing

ChitraFrame offers 4 standard sizes: Small (8×12"), Medium (12×18"), Large (18×24"), and XL (24×36"). All sizes are available in both Framed and Poster print options.
Framed prints come in a premium MDF wooden frame ready to hang on your wall — with glass front and hanging hardware included. Poster prints are high-quality tube-rolled prints without a frame — ideal if you have your own frame or prefer a minimalist look. Framed options cost slightly more.
A White Mount adds a white mat border inside the frame around your print, giving it a premium gallery-style look. It costs ₹250 extra and is available for all sizes.
Absolutely. We print on premium 250gsm art paper using high-resolution giclée printing for vivid, fade-resistant colours. Frames are made from solid MDF with a glass front and wall-hanging hardware included.
Currently all frames come in a classic black finish, which complements all our designs beautifully. White mount add-on is available to enhance the gallery look.

Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping is ₹99. Orders above ₹899 qualify for FREE shipping across India. We deliver pan-India including all major cities and towns.
Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days across India. Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) may receive orders faster. We'll send you a tracking link via WhatsApp and email once your order is shipped.
Once your order ships, we send a tracking link via WhatsApp and email. You can also log in to your account on ChitraFrame to check order status in real time.
Currently we ship within India only. International shipping is being explored for the future. Stay tuned on Instagram @chitraframe.in for updates.
