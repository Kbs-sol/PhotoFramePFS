# ChitraFrame Instagram Marketing Strategy
**Handle**: @chitraframe.in  
**Platform**: Instagram (primary), YouTube Shorts (secondary)  
**Goal**: Build brand awareness for premium photo frames & wall art in India, drive traffic to chitraframe.in  
**Target**: 10K followers in 6 months, 500 monthly orders via Instagram attribution  

---

## 1. Target Audience Profiles

### Primary Segments

#### 🏠 Segment A — Home Decorators (35% of audience)
- **Age**: 25–40, predominantly female
- **Location**: Tier-1 & Tier-2 cities — Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Indore
- **Interests**: Interior design, home improvement, Myntra/Nykaa/Pepperfry shoppers, Pinterest users
- **Pain point**: Can't find affordable, aesthetic wall art that isn't generic IKEA posters
- **Trigger**: "How to decorate living room walls in India"
- **Content hook**: Room makeover reveals, before/after wall styling, "how to style your wall"

#### 🙏 Segment B — Spiritual Gifters (30% of audience)
- **Age**: 30–55, mixed gender
- **Location**: Pan-India, strong in Gujarat, Rajasthan, UP, Maharashtra
- **Interests**: Temple visits, devotional content, festival shopping, WhatsApp family groups
- **Pain point**: Generic pooja room art that feels cheap; no premium spiritual wall art
- **Trigger**: Diwali gifting, housewarming, birthdays of parents/elders
- **Content hook**: Pooja room transformation, "best gift for mom this Diwali", Navratri/Ram Navami content

#### ⚽ Segment C — Sports Fans (20% of audience)
- **Age**: 18–30, predominantly male
- **Location**: Pan-India, urban centres
- **Interests**: Football (EPL, La Liga), F1, cricket (but not our catalog), men's lifestyle
- **Pain point**: No premium sports art that looks gallery-quality, not just a printed photo
- **Trigger**: World Cup wins, match victories, gifting for fan friends
- **Content hook**: "Upgrade your room with this Messi print", F1 car art, "every football fan needs this"

#### 🚗 Segment D — Car & Bike Enthusiasts (15% of audience)
- **Age**: 18–35, male
- **Location**: Urban metros, tier-2 cities
- **Interests**: Superbikes, F1, Porsche, BMW, CarToq, ThrottleQuest
- **Pain point**: No dedicated automotive wall art brand in India at accessible prices
- **Content hook**: Porsche 911 neon art, F1 Monaco night print, BMW M4 track
