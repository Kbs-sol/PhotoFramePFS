## Recommendation

### Keep ChitraFrame ✅

**Reasons:**
1. **Meaning is perfect:** "Chitra" (चित्र) = picture, painting, image in Sanskrit/Hindi — exactly what you sell
2. **Unique in the market:** No other Indian framing/art brand uses this name
3. **SEO advantage:** "chitraframe" is a specific, uncompeted search term — you OWN it from day one
4. **Pronunciation memorability:** Once heard, remembered — and it SOUNDS Indian (authentic positioning)
5. **Domain:** chitraframe.in is clean, brandable
6. **Tagline fit:** "Art that speaks. Frames that last." — works perfectly

### If Pivoting Away

If you decide to rebrand, the strongest alternative is:

> **WallStory.in** — "Every wall tells a story" 
> - Generic enough to scale beyond frames
> - Great narrative marketing angle
> - Short, memorable, no pronunciation friction
> - Tagline: "Your walls. Your story."
